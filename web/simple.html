Simple mode

Your lineups in four taps.

Upload the entries file from DraftKings. We read your contests and build a lineup for every entry from 10,000 simulated games.

Where do I get the entries file?
  1. Enter your Classic contests on DraftKings as usual (empty lineups are fine).
  2. Go to My Contests → Upcoming, choose the slate, and click Edit Entries.
  3. Click Download CSV. That file is your DKEntries.csv — drop it above.

No entries yet? Download the salaries CSV from any contest's lobby (Export to CSV) and drop that instead — you'll choose how many lineups you want.
