Your first build (60 seconds)

  1. Open the Lineup Builder. This week's DraftKings main slate is already loaded.
  2. Under Contest type, choose Cash for 50/50s and double-ups, or Tournament for GPPs.
  3. Under How many lineups?, pick the number of entries you're playing (1, 3, 20, 50 or 150, or type any number). The builder applies settings that suit that contest size.
  4. Press Build lineups. You'll land on the Lineups tab with your lineups, their simulated ranges and your exposure to every player.
  5. Press Download CSV and upload the file on DraftKings (see below).

That's it. Everything else here is about making the lineups more yours.

Cash vs tournament

Cash games (50/50s, double-ups, head-to-heads) pay roughly the top half of the field, so you want a lineup that scores well most of the time. Cash lineups lean toward safe floors and are ranked by their simulated median.

Tournaments pay a small slice of the field, so you need lineups that can reach the top. Tournament lineups stack correlated players, add a little randomness so they don't all look alike, and are ranked by their simulated 90th percentile: the score they reach in their best 1-in-10 outcomes.

Locks, excludes & edits

  • Lock (the padlock) forces a player into every lineup.
  • Exclude (the circle-slash) keeps a player out of every lineup.
  • Edit a projection by clicking the number in the Proj column and typing your own. Edited numbers turn amber and flow into every lineup.
  • Edit ownership the same way if you have a better estimate.

Your locks, excludes and edits are remembered in this browser for the slate. The My picks filter shows only the players you've touched, and Clear all resets them. Click any player's name for his full player card.

Stacking

A stack pairs your quarterback with his own pass catchers: when the passing game goes off, the whole group scores together. QB + 1 requires at least one teammate WR/TE with the QB; QB + 2 requires two. Bring-back adds at least one player from the opposing team, betting that the game turns into a shootout.

The Team stacks tab ranks every quarterback with his top pass catchers. Lock stack locks the QB and his two best receivers into every lineup in one click.

Exposure & diversity

Max exposure caps how often any one player appears across your lineups. At 40% with 20 lineups, nobody appears in more than 8. Lower numbers spread your risk; higher numbers concentrate on your favorite plays. Locked players ignore the cap.

For player-level control, switch the table's columns to Portfolio. There you can set a Min % (force a player into at least that share of lineups) or a Max % for anyone. Changes there re-pick from the lineups already built, with no rebuild needed.

Fine-tune holds the rest: minimum salary used, max players per team, minimum unique players between lineups, randomness, an ownership fade, a cap on total lineup ownership, the candidate pool size, and how much projections lean on the betting markets. Reset to preset puts everything back.

Reading your lineups

Every lineup card shows its projection and its simulated range. Because every player's score comes from the same simulated games, a lineup's range already accounts for correlation: a QB and his receiver rise and fall together, and a defense suffers when the opposing offense booms.

10th percentile
A bad day. The lineup scores below this 1 time in 10.
Median
The middle outcome. Cash lineups are ranked by this.
90th percentile
A great day, reached 1 time in 10. Tournament lineups are ranked by this.

The Exposure panel shows how much of your portfolio each player takes up (green bar) next to how much of the field is projected to play him (tick mark).

Uploading to DraftKings

  1. In the builder, press Download CSV. The file has one lineup per row, using DraftKings player IDs in the order QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, WR, TE, FLEX, DST.
  2. On DraftKings (desktop site), go to Lineups and choose Upload lineups. Pick NFL, this slate, and the file.
  3. Enter the uploaded lineups into your contests. To change lineups you've already entered, use Edit entries on the contest and upload in the same format.

Late news? Exclude the player, rebuild, and upload again before his game locks.

When numbers update

Stats, injuries, depth charts, weather and betting lines refresh every night at 5 AM ET, again Thursday and Saturday evenings, and at 7:00, 10:00 and 11:45 AM ET on Sundays. The badge next to the slate picker shows when the simulations last ran; reload the page after an update to pick it up.

Glossary

Proj
Projected fantasy points: the average of the simulated outcomes, blended with betting-market player props where they're posted.
Value
Projected points per $1,000 of salary.
Floor / Ceiling
The 10th and 90th percentile of the player's simulated outcomes.
Boom / Bust
The chance of a big game or a dud relative to his salary.
Own%
Projected share of the contest field that will play the player.
Exposure
The share of your lineups the player is in.
Leverage
Exposure minus projected ownership. Positive means you're over the field on him.
Pool %
How often the player showed up across every candidate lineup the builder considered: the simulations' own preference.
Ranges (25th–99th)
Points at each percentile of the player's simulated outcomes.
