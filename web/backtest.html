Projection accuracy

Each past week is replayed using only what was known before kickoff, then compared with what happened. Our projections sit next to two simpler methods, a recent-form estimate and a plain season average, so you can see whether ours add anything.

Are the ranges honest?

Every projection comes with a range. This shows where real scores landed inside it. Bars close to the dashed line mean the ranges are about right. Tall bars at the edges would mean the ranges are too narrow.
