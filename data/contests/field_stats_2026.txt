########## Takeaways (2026 wk1 831k + wk2 Milly, consistent with 14 weeks of 2020)
  - Field stacks the QB 85-87% of the time, ~38-40% with a bring-back. Top 1%: 92% stacked, 40-63% bring-back, bigger stacks (1.4-1.55 mates).
  - Winning lineups carry MORE summed ownership than the field (+8 to +16), not less. They ride the chalk that hits (Gibbs 43% -> 37.6) and
    differentiate with 1-2 pieces (Love 2%, Watson 4%, Moore 4%). Hard chalk fades cost more than they save.
  - Scoring level moves a lot week to week (median 114 -> 142), so cash/1% lines must come from the slate's own sims, not fixed numbers.
