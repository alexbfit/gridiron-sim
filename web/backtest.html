Each past week is replayed with only the data available before kickoff (closing Vegas lines, prior game logs, players who dressed). Three models are scored against actual DraftKings points for DFS-relevant players: the Monte Carlo sim, the baseline model, and a naive season-to-date average.

Accuracy by position

MAE = mean absolute error in DK points (lower is better). r = correlation with actuals (higher is better). Bias = projection − actual (0 is calibrated). The sim's mean is compared; its median scores like the baseline on MAE because point scores are right-skewed.
