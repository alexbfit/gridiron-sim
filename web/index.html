FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does the beta cost?

Nothing. No credit card. When paid plans launch you'll get an email first, and beta players keep founding-member pricing. If you'd rather not continue, you simply don't subscribe.

Which sites and slates are supported?

DraftKings NFL classic main slates today. FanDuel and showdown slates are next.

How do I get my lineups onto DraftKings?

Build, then press Download CSV. On DraftKings, use Upload lineups for new entries or Lineups → Edit Entries to replace lineups you've already entered.

Can I use my own opinions?

Yes. Edit any projection, lock or exclude any player, and set minimum or maximum exposure. Your changes flow into every lineup.

When will I get access?

We're letting players in in small groups so we can help each one get set up. You'll get an email with your link when your spot opens.

Will this guarantee I win?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. Daily fantasy has real variance and a rake. GameTime Win gives you calibrated projections and disciplined lineup construction, and publishes its own results every week so you can judge for yourself. Only play with money you can afford to lose.
