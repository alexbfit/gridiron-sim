Your Sunday in three steps

  1. Enter your contests on DraftKings. Reserve your entries as usual. Empty lineups are fine. Then go to My Contests, Upcoming, click Edit Entries and click Download CSV. That is your entries file.
  2. Build your lineups here. Open Lineups and drop the entries file in. Choose at least two players you want more of or none of (DraftKings requires your own input before any tool builds for you), then press Build.
  3. Upload the finished file. Press Download for DraftKings. Back on DraftKings, go to My Contests, Upcoming, Edit Entries, choose Upload CSV and pick the file. Every entry is filled.

No entries yet? Open any contest on DraftKings, click Export to CSV to get the salaries file, and drop that in instead. You choose how many lineups you want and upload them with Upload Lineups.

Late scratches

Players are sometimes ruled out about 90 minutes before kickoff. Come back before the first game and before the late afternoon games. If someone in your lineups is out, build again and upload the new file. DraftKings only lets you change players whose games haven't started.

The advanced builder

Advanced builder does the same job as the quick build with every setting open to you.

  • Lock (the padlock) puts a player in every lineup. Exclude (the circle with a line) keeps him out of all of them.
  • Change a projection by clicking the number in the Proj column and typing your own.
  • Stacking pairs your quarterback with his own receivers, so the lineup scores together when that passing game has a big day. QB + 2 means two of his receivers. The switch below it adds one player from the other team.
  • Most lineups one player can be in spreads your risk. At 35% with 20 lineups, nobody is in more than 7.
  • More settings holds the rest. Reset to our defaults puts everything back.

Your locks, excludes and changes are remembered in this browser for the week.

The other pages

Players
Every player's numbers by week or for the season. Click a column to sort.
Props
Where our numbers and the betting markets disagree about a player. Use it as a list of things to look into. A big gap usually means news.
Results
How each Sunday's lineups did against the rest of the contest, and how close our projections were. Good weeks and bad weeks are both there.

When numbers update

Injuries, weather and betting lines refresh every morning, again on Saturday evening, and several times on Sunday morning up to about 15 minutes before the first kickoff. The Home page shows when projections were last updated. Reload a page to pick up the newest numbers.

Words we use

Proj
Projected fantasy points.
Value
Projected points for every $1,000 of salary.
Floor and ceiling
A bad day and a great day for the player. He lands below the floor about 1 time in 10 and above the ceiling about 1 time in 10.
Typical day, great day
The same idea for a whole lineup: its middle outcome, and the score it reaches about 1 time in 10.
Own% or Drafted
The share of the contest's lineups that have the player.
Exposure
The share of your lineups that have the player.
Tournament
A contest with a big prize pool where only the top finishers win much. DraftKings calls these GPPs.
Cash game
A contest that pays about half the entries the same amount, like a 50/50 or a double-up.

Membership and billing

Membership is billed monthly and renews until you cancel. To change your card, get an invoice or cancel, open the Account menu at the top right and choose Account and billing. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Projections are estimates and no lineup is guaranteed to win. Only enter contests with money you can afford to lose. See Responsible play.
