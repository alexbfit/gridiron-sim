Which sport?

NFL uses our own projections. For any other sport you also upload a projections file (a spreadsheet with a name column and a projection column).

Upload your DraftKings entries file

We read your contests and build a lineup for every entry.

Where to find the file

  1. Enter your contests on DraftKings as usual. Empty lineups are fine.
  2. Go to My Contests, then Upcoming, and click Edit Entries.
  3. Click Download CSV. That file is the one to drop above.

No entries yet? Open any contest on DraftKings, click Export to CSV to get the salaries file, and drop that instead. You then choose how many lineups you want.
