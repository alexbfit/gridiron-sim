FAQ

What's the catch?

Why is this $29 when SaberSim is $97 and Stokastic is $150+?

They run media companies, Discords and sales teams. We run a simulator and publish the receipts. We'd rather have a thousand players at a price they call a no-brainer than a hundred at a price they resent — and the founding price is how we thank the people who show up first.

Will this guarantee I win?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. Daily fantasy has real variance and a rake. What we guarantee is better-than-average lineups, judged on the public ledger — see the Beat-the-Field Guarantee above. Only play with money you can afford to lose.

Do you upload to DraftKings for me?

No. You always make the final calls and you always do the upload. You pick at least two players yourself on every build (DraftKings requires it) and you can edit anything. We hand you the file; you press the button.

Which sites and slates are supported?

DraftKings NFL main slates (the Sunday 1 PM classic slate) today. Showdown, FanDuel and the NBA are next; the engine already backtests every major sport.

What does the beta cost, really?

Nothing. No credit card. When paid plans open you'll get an email first; if you'd rather not continue, you simply don't subscribe. See the plans →

When will I get access?

We let players in in small groups so each one gets set up properly. You'll get an email with your link when your spot opens — usually within a few days.

Which states?

Wherever DraftKings offers paid DFS. It isn't available in HI, ID, MT, NV or WA, and a few states restrict certain contest types. Details and responsible-play resources →
