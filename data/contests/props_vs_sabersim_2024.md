Random entry = 0.5 top-1% per 50 lineups (0.2 per 20, 0.4 per 40). Dollar ROIs are driven by single hits (props 1.0 had a rank-2 Millionaire finish in week 2; props .85 a $750 Play-Action prize) — read avg pts, top-1% and median best rank.
- **The live setup (props .85 + no-market discount) vs SaberSim: +10.6 pts per lineup, top-1% lineups 2.93 vs 0.77 per week, median best rank 240 vs 1,935, top-1000 in 25 vs 13 of 30.** Props .85 > props 1.0 (+1.9 ± 0.8 pts: keeping 15% of the sim helps). The no-market discount: +1.0 ± 0.5 pts on top of props .85 (+0.8 ± 0.5 on props 1.0). Adding SaberSim on top of props did not help (−1.0 pt, top-1% 2.17 vs 2.77).
- Why: sportsbook lines rate player value better. 2,297 player-weeks with props: r(actual) props .596 / SaberSim .575 / our sim .484; value over salary r .419 / .373 / .219. Biggest gap at RB (.447 vs .371 — SaberSim over-projects RBs by +0.9 pts) and WR (.370 vs .296). SaberSim is better only at QB (.304 vs .243).
- Leak checks on the props dump (it has no timestamps): anytime-TD prices are calibrated like pregame lines (implied 30–40% → 31% scored; AUC .64 among starters), and props' edge over SaberSim is the same for 1 PM and late games (r gap +.026 vs +.014) — a mid-afternoon snapshot would show a big 1 PM gap. Live lines are pulled at 7 AM Sunday, a little less sharp than closing lines.
- Rank groups with props .85: every group #1–50 was profitable in both the Play-Action and the mini-MAX (with SaberSim or sim only, only #1–20 were).
- **Correlation calibration** (`--corr`, e03eda1): the sim's player-pair correlations vs 2024–25 outcomes (~750 team-weeks, residual = actual − SaberSim proj): it under-links QB–WR1/WR2 (.27/.22 vs real .39/.38), QB–opp QB (−.02 vs .13), WR1–opp WR1 (.02 vs .12), and over-links QB–own RB1 (.21 vs .04), QB–own DST (+.10 vs −.08), RB1–RB2 (+.03 vs −.08); same direction both seasons. `apply_corr` moves each game's draws toward the real values (Gaussian copula, every player's distribution unchanged; deltas fit on the other season). Contest result: neutral in all four setups (−0.4 / −0.6 / 0.0 / −0.2 pts per lineup, top-1% flat), because the builder already forces QB+2 + bring-back and correlation only re-ranks candidates. Committed, off by default.
- Distribution check (6,214 player-weeks): sim quantiles roughly calibrated (share above p85 17.7% vs 15% ideal, above p95 6.5% vs 5%); WR upper tail a little thin, QB upper tail a little wide, DST top tail thin (3.8% above p99). Not acted on.
- **Play-Action designs (20 lineups):** a separate 20-lineup build = lineups #1–20 of the 50 build (+0.12 ± 0.25 top-1%/wk). Max-exp .6 / min-uniq 3: no better. **QB+3:** the first run (props 1.0) showed +0.69 ± 0.19 top-1%/wk including a $25,000 Play-Action win, but it did not repeat: props .85 +0.12 ± 0.23 (30 cells), 2025 SaberSim +0.14 ± 0.15 (46 cells, 2024–26), and −2.5 to −3.8 pts per lineup. Not adopted. Real fields do favor QB+3 per lineup — top-0.1% over-representation 1.4–1.6× vs 1.1–1.25× for QB+2, in every contest and season (Play-Action 2025: QB+3 5.6% of field → 8.0% of top 0.1%; QB+2 32.9% → 39.9%) — so revisit as weeks accumulate.
- QB+3 for the whole 50 (live setup): worse — −5.4 ± 1.2 pts per lineup, Millionaire top-1% 1.77 vs 2.93/wk, mini-MAX #1–40 top-1% 1.62 vs 2.38/wk; only the Play-Action #1–20 was a hair better (1.31 vs 1.25). Not adopted.
