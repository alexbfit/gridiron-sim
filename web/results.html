What the owner columns mean

Consensus view: every lineup, ours and every real entry in the contest standings, is re-scored in the same simulated games on neutral market projections. Our own opinions are removed, so it measures how well the lineups are put together against what the field actually played. Expected ROI after rake; the field averages roughly −15% to −25%.

Dupes: exact copies of our lineups among the real entries. A prize is split across copies.

Real $: the owner's own entries in the same contest, matched by DraftKings username in the standings export. A reconstruction from the standings file, not an account statement.
