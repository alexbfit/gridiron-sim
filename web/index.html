What's inside

A simulation engine, not a spreadsheet.

Most optimizers add up average projections. Gridiron Sim simulates the games themselves, so the lineups understand how players win together.

Monte Carlo game sim

Every game is played out 10,000 times from the Vegas spread and total — pace, game script, targets, carries and touchdowns — so every player gets a full range of outcomes, not one number.

Market-anchored projections

Sportsbook and prediction-market player props are blended into projections. In testing on 2024 data they predicted fantasy points better than any model alone.

Correlation built in

A QB and his receivers come from the same simulated game, so stacks are priced honestly. Lineups are ranked by their simulated median (cash) or ceiling (tournaments).

Portfolio controls

Max exposure, per-player minimums and maximums, uniqueness, bring-backs, salary floors and leverage against projected ownership — for when you want full control.

Player cards

Click any player to see his simulated distribution, market lines, floor and ceiling, boom and bust odds, and recent game log.

Always current

Stats, injuries, depth charts, weather and betting lines refresh nightly, and again Thursday and Saturday evening and three times on Sunday morning before lock.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which sites and slates are supported?

DraftKings NFL classic main slates today. The builder already speaks FanDuel's roster and salary rules; FanDuel slates and showdown are next on the list.

How do I get my lineups onto DraftKings?

Build, then press Download CSV. In DraftKings open the contest, choose Upload lineups (or Edit lineups → Upload for entries you already have) and pick the file. Each row is one lineup. The quick-start guide walks through it.

When are projections updated?

Every night at 5 AM ET, again Thursday and Saturday evenings, and at 7:00, 10:00 and 11:45 AM ET on Sundays so final injury designations and line moves are in before lock. The builder shows when the simulations last ran.

Can I use my own projections or opinions?

Yes. Click any projection or ownership number to change it — your edits flow into every lineup and are remembered in your browser. You can also lock, exclude and set minimum or maximum exposure for any player.

What's the difference between cash and tournament settings?

Cash lineups aim for a high, safe score and are ranked by their simulated median. Tournament lineups chase the ceiling: they stack a quarterback with his receivers, add randomness for variety, and are ranked by their simulated 90th percentile.

Will this guarantee I win?

No. Nobody can promise that — daily fantasy has real variance and a rake. Gridiron Sim gives you well-calibrated projections and disciplined lineup construction, and shows its own results every week so you can judge for yourself. Only play with money you can afford to lose.
